Privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026. Applies to the Paceline Chrome extension and this website.

Summary

Paceline works on your device. It has no backend, no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. We do not collect, receive or sell your personal data.

What the extension processes

  • Draft post text on x.com and twitter.com. Compose Guard reads the text you are typing to check it for solicitation phrases and to show a badge. This happens in your browser. The text is not stored and not transmitted.
  • CSV files you import. The Pace Dashboard parses files you choose from X Analytics in your browser. The imported figures and your settings (calibration numbers, follower count, Premium checkbox) are saved in your browser's local extension storage so they are there next time. They are never uploaded.
  • Settings. Compose Guard preferences are kept in Chrome's extension storage, which may sync across your own signed-in Chrome browsers through your Google account.

License check

If you buy Pro, you enter a license key. Paceline sends the key and the product ID to Gumroad (api.gumroad.com) to confirm it is valid. This request does not count a use against your key. Your key, the result, a masked version of your email and the time of the last check are stored locally so Paceline can recheck periodically. Gumroad handles payment and processes your data under its own privacy policy. Paceline never sees your payment details.

Permissions

  • storage: saves your settings, imported data and license state.
  • x.com and twitter.com: runs Compose Guard on the compose box.
  • api.gumroad.com: the license check described above.

What we do not do

  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No advertising, tracking or profiling.
  • No remote code. All code ships inside the extension package.
  • No reading of your timeline, messages or account credentials.

Your control

Removing the extension deletes its local data. You can also clear imported data by clearing the extension's storage in Chrome.

Website

This website is static. It sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. Your host (for example GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs under its own policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
